Contact KEMS AI

Contact KEMS Artificial Intelligence to discuss enterprise causal intelligence, request a demonstration, ask a technical question or explore how KEMS could connect to your operational environment.

Sales and demonstrations

Email sales@kemsai.co.uk for product evaluation, demonstrations, commercial enquiries and conversations about potential use cases. To help the team respond effectively, describe the operational problem you are trying to solve, the approximate scale of your environment and the kinds of topology or telemetry systems already in use. Do not email passwords, access tokens or other production credentials.

Technical support and integrations

Email support@kemsai.co.uk for technical support, deployment questions and current integration information. Include a concise description of the issue, the relevant KEMS deployment level and any non-sensitive error message that helps explain the problem. Remove confidential infrastructure details and secrets before sending diagnostic information.

Business location and response

KEMS Artificial Intelligence is based in London, United Kingdom. The website states that the team aims to respond to enquiries within 24 hours. Response times may vary depending on the question and availability. Public API credentials and private deployment access are not distributed through this page.
