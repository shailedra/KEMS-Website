About KEMS AI

KEMS Artificial Intelligence develops causal intelligence software for enterprise operations. KEMS is designed to help technology teams move from large volumes of disconnected alerts and records to a clearer explanation of what happened, what was affected and what action should be considered next.

The problem KEMS addresses

Operational knowledge is commonly divided between monitoring platforms, configuration records, incident histories and the experience of different teams. During an incident, people must manually reconstruct how systems depend on one another while signals continue to arrive. This fragmentation can make root-cause analysis slow, inconsistent and difficult to explain.

How KEMS approaches the problem

KEMS combines operational topology and telemetry in a unified dependency model. That model supports evidence-backed root-cause analysis, blast-radius and impact analysis, predictive operations and simulation of proposed changes before they reach production. A natural-language interface can help users ask questions about causes, dependencies and impact without replacing the underlying evidence.

Where KEMS fits

KEMS is intended to sit above an organisation’s existing operational stack rather than require teams to replace their monitoring, CMDB or telemetry systems. It is relevant to operations teams, platform engineers and service owners who need to understand relationships across a complex technology estate. Current deployment, integration, security and commercial details should be confirmed directly with KEMS.
